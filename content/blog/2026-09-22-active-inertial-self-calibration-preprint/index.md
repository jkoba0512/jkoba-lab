Our new preprint, "Active Inertial Self-Calibration Beyond Identifiability: Task-Compatible Excitation and Terminal Feasibility," was posted on SSRN on September 22, 2026.

A robot can accurately estimate its dynamics and still fail to complete a movement under torque, time, and velocity limits. This study separates inertial self-calibration into three layers: identifying the parameters that can be estimated, choosing informative motions that preserve the task, and assessing whether the final maneuver is feasible. Simulations on planar arms and a seven-degree-of-freedom KUKA LBR iiwa show that a velocity-aware terminal-feasibility risk predicts task failure better than calibration error alone.

This is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.

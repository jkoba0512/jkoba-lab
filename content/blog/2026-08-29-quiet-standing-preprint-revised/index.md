A revised version (v4) of our bioRxiv preprint on quiet standing is now available: "A Mathematical Model of Reliability-Dependent Sensory Reweighting in Quiet Standing."

The revised analysis includes intrinsic ankle stiffness and derives the steady posture and a linear stability condition from sensory precisions, goal dynamics, and stiffness. Across 115 closed-loop trials, settled equilibria matched the predictions to within 0.001 degrees. An eightfold reduction in an unreliable channel's precision reduced perturbation-driven posture shifts by 82%; intrinsic stiffness left this reduction essentially unchanged for visual and sensory-only perturbations but bounded it for physical support tilts. A within-trial precision switch further showed that belief updating carries the change to ankle torque, without a separate action gain.

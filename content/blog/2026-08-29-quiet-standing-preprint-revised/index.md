A revised version (v3) of our bioRxiv preprint on quiet standing is now available, retitled "A Mathematical Model of Reliability-Dependent Sensory Reweighting in Quiet Standing."

The manuscript has been restructured into a theory-first format. A new fixed-point analysis derives closed-form predictions for the perturbation-induced belief bias, each sensory channel's contribution to the state update, the resulting closed-loop posture bias with a stability condition on the upright goal, and the reduction ratios; the simulation experiments then verify them. The appendix derivations are expanded, and a stochastic observation-noise robustness check has been added.

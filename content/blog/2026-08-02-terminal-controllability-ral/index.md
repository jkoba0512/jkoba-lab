Our paper, "When Identifiability Is Not Enough: Terminal Controllability in Task-Compatible Active Calibration," appears in *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, vol. 11, no. 10, pp. 11039–11045 (October 2026).

A robot that explores to calibrate its own body can end up with accurate parameters and still fail the task it was calibrating for. The paper shows that the exploratory motion also decides whether the robot is left in a state from which the task can still be completed, and separates active calibration into three layers: identifiability creation, task-compatible exploration, and post-calibration finite-horizon controllability.
